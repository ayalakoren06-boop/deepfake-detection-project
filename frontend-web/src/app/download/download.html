Install the AI fake detection extension

Just 3 simple steps stand between you and safe browsing, with detection of AI-generated face images right from your browser.

1

Download the extension file

Click the button below to download the installation file (ZIP) to your computer. After downloading, extract the files to an accessible folder.

2

Quick install in Chrome

The extension currently runs in developer mode. To install it in seconds:

  • Open a new browser tab and go to: chrome://extensions/
  • Turn on the Developer mode switch in the top corner.
  • Click Load unpacked and select the folder you extracted in the previous step.
3

Scan faces with a right-click

The extension is installed and ready! How do you use it?

  • Browse any website or social network.
  • Find a face image you want to verify.
  • Right-click on it.
  • Choose the menu option: "Check authenticity with DeepFence".
Good to know: Every scan you run through the extension is automatically deducted from your monthly scan quota and synced right away with your dashboard on the website.
